# Stakeholders: Campus Concierge

| Stakeholder/role | Need | Concern | Evidence/access |
|---|---|---|---|
| Requester (student needing an errand run) | Find a willing helper quickly and trust the deal will be honoured | Helper doesn't show, or terms are disputed after the fact | Team members are themselves potential requesters; accessible via peer interviews |
| Helper (vetted student registered to run errands) | Get auto-assigned errands and clear task terms | Unfairly blamed with no record of what was actually agreed | Accessible via peer interviews with students who have informally helped others |
| App admin (helper vetting and assignment) | A reliable pool of helpers, and standard prices per category that stay fair | Not enough available vetted helpers, or a category price that drifts out of step with real effort | Team's own operational role running the vetting and registration process |
| Residence / hall administration | Deliveries and meetups don't disrupt hall or accommodation operations | Unknown visitors arriving at hall entrances for handoffs | Informal conversation with a hall porter about visitor handoffs at the entrance |
| University IT | Identity tied to real, existing student accounts | Anonymous or spoofed accounts enabling abuse | UB student acceptable-use policy (general reference, not errand-specific) |
