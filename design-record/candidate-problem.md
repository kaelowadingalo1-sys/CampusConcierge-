# Candidate project problem

University of Botswana students often need help from several administrative and support units, but information is spread across different channels and it is difficult to know who owns an enquiry or whether it has progressed. Campus Concierge will provide one accessible entry point where students can search approved guidance, submit a structured request, receive a clear route to the responsible service owner, and follow its status. The project must protect personal information, keep service ownership visible, and remain usable on low-bandwidth mobile connections.
