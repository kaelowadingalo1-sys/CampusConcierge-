# Design record - Lab 01

## Evidence that changed our mind

The laboratory brief requires editable, reproducible evidence rather than screenshots. That means our initial model must be stored as an editable source and exported in open review formats.

## Decision made

We will organise the repository around design evidence (`models/`, `glossary/`, `prototype/`, `tests/`, `evidence/`, and `design-record/`) and use an SVG context model as the first editable model.

## Consequence accepted

The team must maintain both source and export files, and every design decision must name its evidence and next uncertainty.

## First risk

Service owners may disagree about request categories and routing responsibilities.

## Next action

Interview or consult one representative from Student Affairs and one academic department to validate the first service categories and escalation rules.
