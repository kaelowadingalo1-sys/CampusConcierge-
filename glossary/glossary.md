# Glossary

| Term | Working definition | Owner / source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campus Concierge | The proposed digital front door for University Service Hub enquiries and requests. | Team | Draft |
| Student | A registered learner who searches for information or submits a service request. | Team | Draft |
| Service request | A trackable request for help, information or a referral to a University unit. | Team | Draft |
| Service owner | A University unit responsible for resolving a request in its area. | Team | Draft |
| Knowledge article | Maintained guidance that can answer a common enquiry without creating a request. | Team | Draft |
| Triage | Routing a request to the appropriate service owner and priority. | Team | Draft |
